# Third-party data

The built-in dictionary (`app/src/main/assets/dictionary.db.gz`, generated by
`tools/build_dictionary.py`) is derived from:

- **Open English WordNet 2024**, © the Open English WordNet contributors and
  Princeton University, licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  <https://en-word.net> · <https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet>.
  Changes: entries were regrouped by headword, definitions and examples were
  capitalized and punctuated, and the data was converted to SQLite.
- **The CMU Pronouncing Dictionary**, © Carnegie Mellon University, BSD
  2-clause licence (<https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict>), used for
  pronunciations not in WordNet. ARPAbet was converted to IPA.

# Icons and artwork

- **Hugeicons** (free stroke-rounded set), MIT License, © Hugeicons
  (<https://hugeicons.com>, <https://github.com/hugeicons/hugeicons>). Used for the
  in-app icons, converted to Android vector drawables by
  `tools/hugeicons_to_vector.py`. Full license text:
  [licenses/hugeicons-MIT.txt](licenses/hugeicons-MIT.txt).
- The launcher icon's "Aa" is drawn from the outlines of **DejaVu Serif Bold**
  (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu fonts license), generated by `tools/make_icon.py`.

Connotations are generated at runtime by Claude (Anthropic) using the user's
own API key and are not part of the bundled data.
