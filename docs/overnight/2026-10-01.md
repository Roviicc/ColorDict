## Stops

- Any blind read at 5% or over, from 50 read senses up. Below 50, every fault
  is repaired third-hand and re-read blind, and the run goes on.
- The instrument gate fails, or a tool fails twice on the same input.
- Agents fail on usage or rate limits twice, ten minutes apart.
- A git lock held by a live process for over 15 minutes.
- The cap of 4 rounds; no round starts after 06:30; everything stops by 08:00.
