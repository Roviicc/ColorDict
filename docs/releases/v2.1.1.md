### Smoother popup
The quick-lookup popup now behaves like a proper bottom sheet, with the sheet and the entry moving as one continuous gesture:

- **Swipe up** and the sheet follows your finger until it's fully open; keep going in the same swipe and the entry scrolls.
- **Swipe down** and the entry scrolls back to the top first, then the sheet follows your finger down to its short position.
- **Swipe down again** from the short position to close it.
- Let go mid-way and it settles smoothly to the nearest position, following the direction of your flick. Back also shrinks an open sheet before closing it.

### Install
Download **colordict-debug.apk** below. It installs as an update over v2.0.0 or v2.1.0 and keeps your saved words and API key.
