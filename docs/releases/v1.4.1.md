# v1.4.1 - stage 10's eight rounds reach the readers

Tag message and release notes. Prepared 2026-10-07 on
`claude/compassionate-bell-d8kn3x`; the author confirmed the number and gave the go
the same day. Main was at v1.4.0.

## What changed in the dictionary

Stage 10, "Close book one", ran eight rounds against Pride and Prejudice
(pg1342): four on 2026-09-26 (734 senses, 0.8% wrong) and four on
2026-10-01 (1,194 senses, 2.7% wrong). Every shipped sense was blind-read by
a reader that had not written it; faults were repaired third-hand and
re-read. The null audit across the night read 2.5%; round r8's own audit read
5.8%, over the 5% gate, on a disagreement about whether approving adjectives
(worthy, wonderful, honourable, superior, nice) carry connotation. That is a
question of the rule, recorded in `data/policy/build-stages.json`
(`stop_2026_10_01`) for the author; r8 ships as read, and its revert is one
overlay file.

Measured against v1.4.0's `batch-0001.jsonl`:

| | v1.4.0 | v1.4.1 |
|---|---|---|
| reviewed headwords | 4,223 | 4,378 |
| senses in the reviewed tier | 15,744 | 16,355 |
| senses with a learner line ("In plain words") | 2,393 | 4,314 |
| senses with examples | 9,715 | 10,984 |
| senses ranked by what the book's reader meets first | 5,211 | 5,913 |
| senses with a tone note | 3,935 | 3,935 |

The tone notes did not move: stage 10 writes the learner line, the examples,
the sense order and the null-or-candidate verdict, never charge or tone. Its
candidates (521 senses in 118 families, `data/families/draft-020.json`) are
the next tone notes, and wait for a go.

Before the 2026-10-01 rounds, the book ingest was corrected: eight tagger
faults (hop/hope, spar/spare, drawing, and five adjectives split across tags)
in `data/policy/ingest-corrections.json`, 24 sentences per lemma, examples
drawn only from sentences the lemma is attested in.
